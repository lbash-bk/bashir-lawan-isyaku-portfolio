About me

I studied Software Engineering at Federal University Dutse and graduated in December 2025 with a CGPA of 4.27/5.00. That training gave me a solid base in programming and databases, and I have focused on applying it to data.

My projects use Python and Pandas to clean and prepare data, and Excel and Power BI to report on it. I have worked with sales data and bank customer data, and I built FUD Study Hub, a Django platform that helps students at my university find academic resources.

Right now I am learning web scraping, so I can collect data from websites instead of only analyzing files I already have. Scikit-learn and machine learning come next. I am looking for an entry-level role in data analysis, reporting or business intelligence.
